Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Syfx ("Syfx," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit syfxfinance.com, join our early-access waitlist, or use the Syfx platform once it launches. Syfx is a non-custodial AI operating system for traders — we never hold, move, or have access to your funds, and that principle shapes how we think about your data too: we collect as little as we reasonably can.

2. Information We Collect

At this stage (pre-launch, waitlist-only), we collect:

  • Email address — when you join the waitlist, so we can contact you about early access and product updates.
  • Basic technical data — standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure.

Once the platform launches, this will expand to include information necessary to operate a non-custodial trading interface, such as:

  • Public wallet addresses you connect to the platform.
  • On-chain activity tied to your vault — trade history, proof receipts, and similar records that are, by design, publicly verifiable on-chain (this is the point of Syfx's zero-knowledge proof system).
  • Usage data to improve the product (feature usage, error logs).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To notify you when early access opens and share relevant product updates.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the platform.
  • To respond to support or contact requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Non-Custodial & On-Chain Data

Because Syfx is non-custodial, your trading activity happens through smart contracts you control, and the resulting records — trades, proofs, wallet addresses — are written to public blockchains and 0G Network. Blockchain data is, by its nature, public and immutable: once it's on-chain, neither Syfx nor anyone else can delete or alter it. This is intentional and central to how Syfx's verifiability works, but it means the usual "right to deletion" concepts don't apply the same way to on-chain records as they do to data stored on our own servers.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the site is used (e.g., aggregate page views) and, where applicable, essential cookies required for the site to function. We do not use invasive cross-site tracking or sell data to ad networks.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of infrastructure providers to operate Syfx, including hosting (Vercel, Railway), and blockchain/proving infrastructure (0G Network, RISC Zero). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy practices.

7. Data Retention

We retain waitlist email addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until they're no longer needed for the purpose collected. On-chain data persists indefinitely as a property of the blockchain itself, independent of Syfx.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal data we hold about you (excluding immutable on-chain records, per Section 4), and to unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any message we send, or by contacting us directly.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data we hold. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to also take standard precautions with your own accounts and wallets — Syfx can never recover funds or reverse transactions on your behalf, since we never have custody or control of them.

10. Children's Privacy

Syfx is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through our contact page.
